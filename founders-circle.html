Why The Founders Circle

Owning a business is rich, demanding and, at times, brutal.

I may have no boss, but I still carry:

  • The weight of payroll, investors and people's livelihoods
  • Decisions I can't fully share with my partner, team or board
  • Questions about exit, succession, or what my next chapter actually is
  • The sense that my identity and my business have quietly fused into one

Friends and family care, but don't always get it.

Talking openly with co-founders or fellow directors rarely feels clean.

I'm not broken. I'm a founder in transition.

The problem is trying to navigate that in isolation.

The Founders Circle is a place to work through the knotty challenges of business and life with other owners who've walked a similar path.

Not a mastermind. Not a networking club.
And absolutely not a place for dick swinging.

Who It's For

Every man in the room:

Owns or has founded a business with meaningful scale (typically £1m+ revenue)

Has led through risk, complexity and pressure

Is usually 40+ and asking harder questions about legacy, family, health and direction

Wants experienced peers who will tell the truth, not an audience to impress

Members may be:

  • Deep in building and scaling
  • Preparing or negotiating an exit
  • Post-exit and working out who they are without "the business"
  • Exploring their next chapter – NED roles, new ventures, or a different pace of life

What we share is simple: we know what it is to be ultimately responsible.

How It Works

Closed group. 10 men max. Same faces, every time.
For 2026, there are 10 seats only – and 4 are still available.

In Person

5 in-person gatherings per year

  • 4 × full-day sessions
  • 1 × overnight in a beautiful setting

Days typically include:

  • Activating breathwork – arrive properly, not just turn up
  • Men's circle – confidential space for what's really going on
  • Nutritious shared food – conversation that actually goes somewhere
  • Walk in nature – space to think without a screen
  • Strategy & legacy work – getting clear on what I'm building now, and why

Between Meetings

  • Group calls in the "off" months to integrate, reset and stay accountable
  • A clear place to bring key decisions and challenges, rather than carrying them alone

Over time, The Founders Circle becomes a blend of:
advisory board, reality check and lifeline.

Am I One of the Four?

A few sharper questions to sit with:

Am I a business owner who is used to being the most responsible man in the room – and the least supported?
Do I secretly know that my current way of operating is unsustainable, even if it looks impressive from the outside?
Have I run out of people I can speak to honestly about the business, my family, my health, and what this is all costing me?
Am I willing to be challenged, held to account, and seen clearly by other serious men – not just admired for my CV?
If the existing group had the final say, would I back myself to be someone they'd want beside them?
